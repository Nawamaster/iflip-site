# Appearance & Skin Studio

## Theme

**Settings → Appearance → Theme** lets you switch between **System**, **Light**, and **Dark** mode using a segmented control. "System" follows your device's overall appearance setting.

## Skin Studio

Skin Studio lets you re-skin iFlip's colors, icons, and background images without changing how the app works — colors, custom background images, 10 saveable preset slots, and export/import as `.iflip-skin` files.

Open it from **Settings → Appearance → Skin Studio**. See the full [Skin Studio guide](./skin-studio.md) for everything you can customize.
