# Accounts

iFlip supports two independent sign‑in options. You can use either, both, or neither — signing in isn't required to use the app.

## Sign in with Apple

- Available from the account row at the top of **Settings**, or from **Settings → Web Drive → Manage your accounts**
- Uses Apple's standard Sign in with Apple flow, requesting your name and email
- Your name/email are only sent by Apple on the very first sign-in and are stored on-device for future sessions
- Tap **Sign Out** to disconnect

## Google Drive Sign‑In

- Sign in from the same account row, from **Settings → Web Drive**, or directly from the **Web Drive** screen when browsing files
- Once signed in, your Google account name/email are shown throughout the app
- Used both for the account row and for [importing files from Google Drive](../importing/from-google-drive.md)
- Tap **Logout** / **Sign Out** to disconnect — this does not delete files you've already imported

## Manage Your Accounts

**Settings → Web Drive → Manage your accounts** shows both providers side by side, each with its own sign-in/sign-out control, so you can review or change either connection without hunting through menus.
