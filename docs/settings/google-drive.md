# Google Drive Settings

Manage your Google Drive connection from **Settings**.

## Connect Google Drive

1. Go to **Settings**
2. Under **Web Drive**, tap the **Google Drive** row (or tap **Sign in** next to it)
3. Sign in with your Google account

## Disconnect Google Drive

1. Go to **Settings**
2. Under **Web Drive**, tap **Logout** next to Google Drive (or open **Manage your accounts** and tap **Sign Out** there)

Disconnecting does not delete files already imported into your library — it only removes the ability to browse Drive again until you sign back in.

## Drive Activity Log

While browsing Google Drive, tap the log icon to see a running **Drive Log** of folder loads, downloads, and any errors — handy for troubleshooting a stuck or failed download.

See [Import from Google Drive](../importing/from-google-drive.md) for the full import walkthrough.
