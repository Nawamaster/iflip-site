# Google Drive Settings

Manage your Google Drive connection in iFlip.

## Connect Google Drive

1. Go to **Settings** tab
2. Tap **Google Drive**
3. Sign in with your Google account

## Disconnect Google Drive

1. Go to **Settings** tab
2. Tap **Google Drive**
3. Tap **Sign Out**

Disconnecting does not delete files already imported into your library.
