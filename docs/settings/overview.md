# Settings Overview

Open **Settings** from the tab bar (iPhone) or sidebar (iPad) to reach every option below.

## Account

A row at the top shows your sign-in status. Tap it to sign in with **Apple** or **Google**, or tap **Sign Out** if you're already signed in. See [Accounts](./accounts.md).

## Web Drive

Shows connection status for **iCloud · Apple** and **Google Drive**, with quick **Sign in** / **Logout** buttons for each. See [Accounts](./accounts.md) and [Import from Google Drive](../importing/from-google-drive.md).

## General

| Item | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **Reading Now** | Customize which sections appear on the Reading Now screen — see [Reading Now](../library/reading-now.md) |
| **Library** | Library-wide toggles, such as preserving the complete route when navigating Favorites |
| **Readers** | Page transition style, per-reader toggles, and paginated/continuous spacing — see [Quick Settings & Reading Preferences](../reading/settings.md) |
| **Manage Space** | Storage usage and cleanup tools — see [Manage Space](./manage-space.md) |
| **Languages** | App display language info and translation feedback — see [Languages](./languages.md) |

## Appearance

- **Theme** — System / Light / Dark, as a segmented control
- **Skin Studio** — customize the app's colors, icons, and background images — see [Appearance & Skin Studio](./appearance-and-skins.md)
- A row of **skin presets** you can save and switch between with a single tap

## Privacy

| Item | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **Password** | Create/change/remove a passcode and enable Face ID for locked content |
| **Locked content** | Related controls for content that's hidden behind your passcode/Face ID |

See [Privacy & Locked Content](./privacy-and-locked-content.md) for details, including how this connects to locking individual collections in your library.

## Contact Us

- **Email us** — opens a pre-filled email to iFlip support
- **iFlip Guides and FAQs** — in-app help (additional guides are added over time)
- **Privacy Policy** — opens iFlip's privacy policy and terms of service in an in-app browser

## About

The bottom of the Settings screen shows the iFlip name, developer contact, and the current app version.
