# Getting Started

iFlip Reader is a personal comic, manga, and book reader for iPhone and iPad. Import your own files and enjoy a clean, distraction-free reading experience.

## Supported Formats

**Comics & Manga**
- CBZ, CBR, CB7, CBT
- ZIP, 7Z, TAR (image archives)

**Documents**
- PDF, EPUB

**Images**
- JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP, HEIC, TIFF, BMP

**Text**
- TXT, MD, CSV

## Ways to Import

- **Files App** — Import directly from your device
- **iCloud Drive** — Browse and import from iCloud
- **Google Drive** — Connect and download from Google Drive
