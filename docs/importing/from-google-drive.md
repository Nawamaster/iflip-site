# Import from Google Drive

Connect your Google account to browse and download files from Google Drive into iFlip.

## How to Connect

1. Go to **Library** tab
2. Tap **Cloud** → **Google Drive**
3. Sign in with your Google account
4. Browse your folders and tap a file to download it

## Notes

- Files are downloaded and saved to your local iFlip library
- This is a manual import — not real-time sync
- You can disconnect Google Drive anytime from **Settings**
