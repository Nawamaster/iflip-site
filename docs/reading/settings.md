# Reader Settings

Customize your reading experience from the quick settings panel.

## Quick Settings

While reading, tap the **center of the screen** → tap the **settings icon** in the top bar.

### Brightness
Adjust screen brightness without leaving the reader.

### Background Theme
Choose your preferred background:
- **White** — standard reading
- **Sepia** — warm tone, easier on the eyes
- **Black** — dark mode reading

## App Settings

Go to **Settings** tab for global options:

### Page Transition
- **Slide** — smooth slide animation (default)
- **Page Curl** — realistic page-turn effect
- **No Effect** — instant transition, lightest on performance

### Display
- **Keep display on while reading** — prevent screen from sleeping
- **Page animations** — enable or disable page turn animations
