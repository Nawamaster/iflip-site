# Organizing Your Library

iFlip gives you tools to keep your library tidy and easy to navigate.

## Collections

Group your files into collections (e.g. Manga, Comics, Novels).

- Tap and hold a book to assign it to a collection
- Collections appear in the sidebar for quick access

## Favorites

Mark your favorite books for quick access.

- Tap the star icon on any book cover
- Access all favorites from the **Favorites** section in the sidebar

## Sorting

Sort your library by:
- **Title** — alphabetical order
- **Recent** — last opened
- **Series** — group by series name
